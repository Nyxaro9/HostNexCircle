The NexCircle model

Five zones. One reason to show up, whoever you are.

Every participant finds their door in. The zones run side by side at every event, so moving between them is as easy as walking across the field.

01

Move

Football, basketball, volleyball, dance, fitness bootcamps, skating & cycling.

02

Create

Photography, video & podcast creation, painting, spoken word, music jams.

03

Build

Coding basics, design, social media marketing, freelancing & entrepreneurship.

04

Chill

Chess, board games, acoustic music, book & movie chats — low-pressure, no performance required.

05

Connect

Speed-friendship sessions, mentorship, networking & team challenges.

Not sure which zone is yours? That's exactly what the Roulette is for.
